## 90-Day Encroachment Plan

*Your partner played the Big Tech attacker. What was their plan to kill you?*

**Attacker:**
open AI 
**Attack vector:**
Launch a native “Enterprise Identity & Risk Screening Copilot” integrated directly into enterprise AI ecosystems with:

multilingual entity resolution
adverse media detection
public figure/PEP identification
open-source intelligence summarization
document and image analysis
workflow copilots for compliance and investigations

The strategy would rely on:

massive model scale
bundled enterprise pricing
existing enterprise trust and procurement channels
rapid deployment with minimal integration effort
**Your defense:**
The defense is to avoid competing on raw AI capability alone and instead become:

the trusted operational decision layer.

Key defensive pillars:

1. Proprietary Operational Intelligence

Build datasets competitors cannot easily access:

analyst adjudications
escalation patterns
false-positive history
multilingual alias resolution outcomes
operational review logic
governance decisions

This becomes the true moat.

2. Human-in-the-Loop Governance

Position the system as:

legally defensible
operationally auditable
policy-aligned
compliant-by-design

Not merely “an AI assistant.”

Focus on:

explainability
traceability
confidence thresholds
review workflows
accountability
3. Deep Workflow Embedding

Integrate tightly into:

notice/diffusion review processes
operational SOPs
analyst tooling
escalation chains
internal databases
compliance processes

The harder it is to remove operationally, the safer the platform becomes.

4. Golden Dataset + Evaluation Leadership

Make evaluation rigor a differentiator.

Demonstrate:

regression testing
hallucination controls
false-positive management
benchmark governance
adversarial testing
audit evidence

Enterprise and public-sector buyers increasingly care more about:

reliability and governance than model novelty.
5. Domain-Specialized Trust

Large AI providers optimize for scale.

Your advantage is:

operational nuance
sensitive-case handling
contextual escalation logic
policy interpretation
institutional trust calibration

Especially in:

law enforcement
compliance
cross-border operational cooperation
regulated intelligence environments
6. Model-Agnostic Architecture

Do not tie the product identity to one model provider.

Instead:

abstract model orchestration
support multiple LLMs
separate reasoning from governance
own the workflow and evaluation layer

This reduces platform dependency risk.

Strategic Principle

If Big Tech owns:

the foundation model

You must own:

the operational trust layer
the adjudication layer
the governance layer
the institutional memory layer
the workflow layer

That is the survivable position.
