## Board Pitch

**Thesis (1 sentence):** Build a trusted AI-assisted operational review platform that accelerates notice screening while strengthening neutrality, human-rights compliance, and governance safeguards.

**The case:**
1. Why now:Operational workloads, open-source complexity, and politically sensitive requests are increasing faster than manual review capacity, while AI capabilities are now mature enough to assist analysts responsibly under strong governance.
2. What's defensible: The defensible layer is not the foundation model itself, but the combination of proprietary operational datasets, analyst adjudication history, governance workflows, golden evaluation datasets, and institutional review logic.
3. The economics:AI-assisted triage and contextual analysis can significantly reduce review time and operational burden while enabling analysts to focus on high-risk and strategically sensitive cases.

**The risks:**
1. Trust / failure modes:False positives, hallucinations, politically biased open sources, and overreliance on AI-generated conclusions could create operational and reputational risks if governance controls fail.
2. Scale / governance:As usage grows, maintaining explainability, auditability, multilingual consistency, and human oversight across jurisdictions becomes increasingly complex.
3. Competitive: Large AI providers may replicate generic screening capabilities, making governance depth, operational integration, and proprietary adjudication intelligence critical for differentiation.

**The ask:** Approve phased investment in AI-assisted operational screening capabilities, including evaluation infrastructure, governance controls, multilingual entity resolution, and human-in-the-loop operational deployment.
